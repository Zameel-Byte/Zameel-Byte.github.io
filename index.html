About me

I'm an Embedded Systems Engineer focused on building hardware and firmware for real-world systems.

My work spans STM32-based embedded systems, PCB design, firmware development, communication protocols, sensor integration, UAV avionics, and robotics. I enjoy working across the complete hardware–software development cycle from schematic and PCB design to firmware, hardware bring-up, debugging, and field testing.

Currently, I work on aerospace and UAV systems, including STM32-based transmitters, MAVLink systems, and custom flight controllers.

I build things, test them, break them, debug them, and make them work.
